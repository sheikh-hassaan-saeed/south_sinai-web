The Destination
BIG DUNE Camp Nuweiba

Welcome to Big Dune Camp, a relaxed Bedouin camp on the shores of the Red Sea in Nuweiba, South Sinai — and a special place for backgammon players.

With the mountains of Sinai behind you and the Red Sea in front, Big Dune offers the perfect setting to enjoy the game in a relaxed and friendly environment. Backgammon has a long and important tradition throughout the Middle East, and here you can enjoy both the familiar Western style of play and the local tradition of Tawla.

Whether you are an experienced tournament player, a regular club player or simply enjoy a game over a coffee or a drink, the camp provides plenty of opportunities to meet other players, play matches and enjoy the social side of backgammon.

Away from the board, there is plenty to enjoy. Swim in the Red Sea, explore the surrounding desert and mountains, relax by the beach, enjoy traditional food or simply sit and watch the spectacular Sinai sunsets.
