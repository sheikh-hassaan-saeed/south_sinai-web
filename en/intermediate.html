The Cubeless Cup

4-Day Tournament for Improving Players

11/11/26 — 18/11/26 • Big Dune Camp, Nuweiba

Tournament Overview

📅 Event Details

  • Level: The Cubeless Cup
  • Venue: Big Dune Camp, Nuweiba
  • Tournament Fee: LE 13,500
  • Registration Fee: FREE
  • Camp Stayer Bonus: 3 Nights & Meals Extra FREE
  • Extra Week Stay: £100 / LE 6,000
  • Prize Breakdown: 1st: 6× Reg Fee (Max LE 37,800 / £540) | 2nd: 3× | 3rd: 2×
  • Trophies: 🏆 Winner & Runner-up Trophies
  • Dates: 11/11/26 – 18/11/26

🎯 Format

  • ✓ Group Stage (Round Robin)
  • ✓ The Cubeless Cup knockout
  • ✓ Supplementary knockout (Consolation)
  • ✓ Minimum 7 matches guaranteed

Scoring Format

  • ✓ Match play with single-point games
  • ✓ Cocked dice must be re-rolled
  • ✓ Dice cups mandatory

Group Stage Structure

Group Composition

Players are divided into groups of four.

16 players → 4 groups

20 players → 5 groups

24 players → 6 groups

Groups scale naturally with player count

Group Format

  • ✓ Round-robin within each group
  • ✓ Each player plays 2 X three matches
  • ✓ Match length: 5 points

Group Standings & Qualification

Ranking Criteria (in order):

  1. 1. Matches won
  2. 2. Head-to-head result
  3. 3. One-game playoff (if still tied and qualification is affected)
  • → Top 2 players from each group advance to The Cubeless Cup knockout
  • → Bottom 2 players go into the supplementary knockout

Knockout Stages

Two parallel single-elimination tournaments

🏆 The Cubeless Cup knockout

Participants:

Top two finishers from each group

Seeding:

  • • Group winners seeded against group runners-up
  • • Players from same group avoid first knockout round if possible

Match Lengths:

First knockout round 5 pts
Quarterfinals 5 pts
Semifinals 7 pts
Final 9 pts

🎯 Supplementary knockout

Participants:

Bottom two finishers from each group

Seeding:

  • • Random draw or mirrored seeding from group results

Match Lengths:

First knockout round 3 pts
Quarterfinals 3 pts
Semifinal 5 pts
Final 7 pts

Four-Day Schedule

1

Day 1 — Group Stage

12/11/26 (Tournament Window: Nov 11–18)

  • • Player briefing and group draw
  • • All group matches (3 rounds)
2

Day 2 — Group Stage

13/11/26

  • • Everyone plays each other again
  • • All group matches (3 rounds)
3

Day 3 — Early knockouts

14/11/26

  • • Main knockout: First round + Quarterfinals
  • • Supplementary knockout: First round
  • • Catch-up matches if required
4

Day 4 — Finals Day

15/11/26

  • • Main knockout: Semi-finals and Final
  • • Supplementary knockout: Semi-final and Final
  • • Awards and closing ceremony

Rules & Administration

  • ✓ Dice: Dice cups mandatory; cocked dice must be re-rolled
  • ✓ Reporting: Winners report scores immediately
  • ✓ Disputes: Tournament supervisor's decision is final

⏰ Late Policy

  • • 10 minutes late: Loss of 1 point
  • • Each additional 5 minutes: Loss of 1 point
