Flights & Travel

Getting to Sharm El-Sheikh & Nuweiba

UK Flights to Sharm El-Sheikh

Nuweiba is easily accessible from Sharm El-Sheikh International Airport (SSH), with direct flights from multiple UK cities.

Direct Flights from the UK

UK Airports to SSH

eJ
easyJet

Regularly operates direct flights LONDON GATWICK (LGW) → Sharm el-Sheikh (SSH). Also operates some Luton (LTN) departures on winter schedules.

Airports with direct EasyJet services to Sharm el-Sheikh:

✈️London Gatwick (LGW)
✈️London Luton (LTN)
✈️Manchester (MAN)
✈️Birmingham (BHX)
✈️Bristol (BRS)
W
Wizz Air UK

Operates direct Luton (LTN) ↔ SSH (and occasionally Gatwick) on winter schedules.

T
TUI

Operates seasonal direct flights from a mix of UK airports when in service; schedule varies by season.

UK airports with direct TUI → Sharm el-Sheikh services:

✈️London Gatwick (LGW)
✈️London Stansted (STN)
✈️London Luton (LTN)
✈️Manchester (MAN)
✈️Birmingham (BHX)
✈️Bristol (BRS)
✈️East Midlands (EMA)
✈️Newcastle (NCL)
✈️Leeds/Bradford (LBA)

Regional airports: Bournemouth (BOH), Exeter (EXT), Norwich (NWI) — TUI may schedule seasonal services to SSH from some of these.

⏱️ Flight Duration

Approximately 5/6 hours direct from UK airports to Sharm El-Sheikh

🚗 Airport Transfer

1.5 hours scenic drive from Sharm El-Sheikh Airport to Big Dune Camp, Nuweiba
